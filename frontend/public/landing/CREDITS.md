## What this means in practice

- **Keep this file** with the images, and keep a visible credit somewhere
  in the shipped site (a footer line is enough) naming the authors and
  licences above.
- The two **CC BY-SA** images carry share-alike terms. Using them
  unmodified as page imagery is fine; if they are cropped, recoloured or
  composited into a new image, that derived image must itself be released
  under the same licence.
- If any of this is inconvenient for the project, `ship-oden.jpg`
  (public domain) has no conditions at all and can replace the others.
